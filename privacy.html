Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy applies to all apps published by Pontus Nyman ("I", "me") on the Apple App Store, and to this website.

Data collection

My apps don't collect, store, or share any personal information unless an app's own section below says otherwise. Data you create in an app stays on your device. If the app supports iCloud, it's synced through your own private iCloud account, which I can't access.

Analytics and crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send me anonymized crash reports and usage statistics. These can't be used to identify you.

Third-party services

My apps don't include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children

My apps don't knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

ScoreBoard Ultra

Team names, colours, rosters and match results are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to me or to any third party.

FastaAppen

Your fasts, fasting history and any weight you choose to enter are stored only on your device. The app shares this data with its own widgets and Live Activity on the same device, so they can show your progress. Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to me or to any third party.

FastaAppen is for general information only and is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a fasting routine.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email pontusnyman@outlook.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll post the update here and change the date at the top.
