Before you write

To help me fix the problem faster, please include:

  • The app name and version (usually found in the app's Settings or About screen)
  • Your device model and iOS/macOS version
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open the app's Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I request a refund?
Apple handles refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
