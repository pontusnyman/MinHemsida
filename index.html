About

I'm Pontus, a developer based just outside Malmö and Lund in southern Sweden.

Before moving into app development, I spent over a decade building test platforms and quality strategies for large products. I've been a QA Architect at Neo4j and a Senior Software Engineer at Qlik, where I worked on Qlik Sense from day one.

Now I'm putting that experience into my own apps: careful engineering, thorough testing, and interfaces that follow Apple's Human Interface Guidelines.
