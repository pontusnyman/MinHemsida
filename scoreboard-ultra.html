ScoreBoard Ultra

A bold, one-tap scoreboard for football, floorball and other team sports. It has a match timer that sounds a buzzer at full time.

Coming soon to the App Store

Everything you need on the sideline

One-tap scoring

Add a goal with a single tap. The score is big and bold, so everyone can see it.

Match timer with buzzer

Set the match length and start the clock. A buzzer sounds when time is up.

Team colours and rosters

Give each team its own colour and add players to the roster.

Match timeline

See every goal in order and when it was scored.

Winner stays on

Run rotations with 3–4 teams. The app keeps track of who plays next.

Private by design

Team names, colours, rosters and match results are stored only on your device.
